## What

Record why a project drain reuses the `cycle_id` identity field instead of renaming it, and file the intake record that routed this work.

## Why

A future maintainer who finds a `cycle_id` holding a project id needs the reason on record. The field is opaque identity at 11 of 13 sites; only `current_cycle_id()` and the `cycle:{id:{eq}}` filter read it as a cycle. Renaming it would touch 29 production and 136 test sites and break existing run-log archives.

## Completion

An ADR at `docs/adrs/0033-project-drain-identity.md` states the decision (overload `cycle_id`, do not rename), the context (site counts, archive compatibility), and the consequences (a cosmetic `target_kind` label fix). The shape:idea triage record exists at `docs/ideas/drain-project-target.md`.

## Assumptions

* ADRs carry no Linear ticket references *(verified)* — repo convention.
* `docs/adrs/` numbering continues at 0033 *(verified)*.

## Key Risks

* The ADR drifts from the code if written after the fact. Mitigation: write it before N03 lands and link the consequence (the label fix) explicitly.
